Agreement Purpose
Purpose

This is a standing agreement between Pi Ops Pty Ltd trading as Principle and Innovation (Pi) and the Installer. It governs the ongoing engagement of the Installer as an independent contractor to install Pi energy systems at properties nominated by Pi.

The Installer's role is to deliver quality workmanship within well-defined job details and expectations.

04
Scope Requirements
Installation Standards
Essential Loads Backup Circuit

Every Pi battery installation must include a default backup circuit covering refrigeration, lighting (minimum one circuit per level), and internet. Confirmed as operational at commissioning sign-off. Variations require Pi's written approval.

Platform Connection

Each system must be commissioned to connect to the Pi/Hardware monitoring platform. Connection confirmed in the Pi Installation Completion Documentation.

Safety Certificate

A Certificate of Compliance – Electrical Work (CCEW) or jurisdiction equivalent must be submitted to Pi systems and lodged with the DNSP as required.

STC Documentation

Pi will supply a digital STC job form for completion. All STCs must be assigned exclusively to Pi Ops Pty Ltd.

Pi Installation Completion Documentation
No payment is made without complete documentation submitted through Pi systems.
  • Pre-install site and full roof condition photos (before any penetrations)
  • During-install compliance photos — terminations, fixings, penetrations before and after sealing
  • Switchboard photos (internal and external), labelling and safety warnings
  • Array overview, panel clamp photos, serial numbers, inverter/battery labels
  • Commissioning photos and data confirming platform connection
  • Completed Pi checklists, safety certificate, STC Assignment Form
  • Voltage rise calculation, shade/site assessment (where applicable)
05
Compliance, Licences & Quality
Installer Obligations
Licences & Accreditations

The Installer and all personnel must hold and maintain:

  • SAA Solar Installer Accreditation (GCPV) and Battery Storage Endorsement (GCBS)
  • State Electrical Contractor Licence and individual Electrician Licence for each worker
  • Applicable state registrations (NSW Fair Trading, QLD ESO, ESV, OTR)
  • Working at Heights Certification, Fall Arrest Safety Training, White Card
  • Any DNSP or regulator-required endorsements
Any lapse must be notified to Pi immediately. Failure results in immediate suspension from work allocation.
Insurance

Maintained at the Installer's cost for the full term:

  • Public Liability ≥ $10,000,000
  • Workers Compensation as required by applicable state legislation
  • Professional Indemnity and tools/equipment insurance where applicable

Insurance must be active from goods collection through job completion. Certificates of Currency on file with Pi before any work is allocated.

Quality Standards

All work must comply with AS/NZS 5033, AS/NZS 5139, AS/NZS 3000, the Pi Installer Compliance Pack, Manufacturer protocols, DNSP technical standards, and SAA guidelines. Pi's system design must be followed without modification — any deviation requires Pi's written approval. Unauthorised changes attract no payment and rectification at the Installer's cost.

10-Year Workmanship Warranty

The Installer provides Pi a 10-Year Workmanship Warranty on all work under this Agreement. Defects must be remediated within:

Critical Safety
24 hrs
Major Defect
3 days
Minor Defect
7 days

Rectification is at the Installer's cost. Manufacturing defects, third-party damage, and normal wear are excluded.

Installer Payment Terms
Payment
Payment Timing
Pi pays within 7 days of receiving a correctly rendered invoice and confirming complete Pi Installation Completion Documentation. Payment is by EFT; AMEX preferred — Installer to provide payment link.
Purchase Orders

Pi issues a Purchase Order for every job before the Installer attends site, confirming scope and rate. No work is to be commenced without a valid PO. Pi will not pay for works performed without one.

Payment Conditions

Payment requires all of the following:

  • Complete documentation with no outstanding items
  • No unresolved compliance breaches
  • Current licences, accreditations, and insurance on Pi's file
  • No outstanding rectification obligations
Deductions

Pi may deduct Audit Rectification Costs from amounts owed or withhold outstanding payments until rectification is resolved. Pi will provide written notice of any deduction.

Terms & Conditions
General Terms
Independent Contractor

The Installer is an independent contractor. Nothing creates employment, agency, partnership, or joint venture. The Installer has no authority to bind Pi.

Customer Ownership, Non-Solicitation & Non-Circumvention

All customers engaged through or introduced by Pi belong exclusively to Pi. The Installer must not, directly or indirectly, solicit, accept private work from, or contact any Pi customer in connection with energy systems, electrical works, or related services — except as expressly directed by Pi — during the term of this Agreement and for 36 months following its termination.

Pre-Existing Relationships. Where the Installer holds a documented pre-existing relationship with a customer established prior to that customer becoming engaged with Pi, private works for that customer outside the Pi engagement are not prohibited, subject to the following conditions:

  • (a) The pre-existing relationship must be disclosed to Pi in writing before any such works are undertaken, identifying the customer and nature of the prior engagement;
  • (b) No work performed by the Installer — whether under the pre-existing relationship or otherwise — shall interfere with, modify, alter, compromise, or adversely affect any Pi energy system, equipment, installation, monitoring platform, or service agreement, unless Pi provides prior written consent; and
  • (c) The Installer must not, under any circumstances, circumvent Pi's contractual arrangements, financial interests, customer relationship, or service obligations with that customer in connection with any Pi-related system, equipment, or service. This includes (without limitation) directly or indirectly accepting work that would displace, replicate, or interfere with services Pi has agreed to provide or is providing to that customer.

Any variation to these obligations must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties before the relevant works commence. Oral arrangements or claims of pre-authorisation are of no effect.

Confidentiality & IP

All Pi designs, data, pricing, documentation, processes, IP, and customer information are confidential and remain Pi's exclusive property. The Installer gains no rights outside this Agreement. This obligation survives termination.

Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.

Variation & Whole Agreement

Variations require written sign-off by both parties. This Agreement, together with the Pi Installer Code of Conduct, Compliance Pack, Site Audit Framework, Certified Partnership Program, and Installer Fee Schedule, constitutes the entire agreement.
