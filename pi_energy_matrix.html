ⓘ Important Notes

  • GST method: GST (10%) is applied to all supplier costs first (inverter, misc, labour, batteries). The STC rebate is then deducted from the GST-inclusive total. STCs are a government incentive and are not subject to GST.
  • Quoted OOP variance: The small difference ($157 for 10kW / $213 for 30kW) between the calculated OOP and Pi Energy's quoted OOP is minor and may reflect rounding or a small unlisted cost. The quoted figures are used as the base in the battery matrix above.
  • Battery add-ons: Battery costs are GST-exclusive from the supplier; 10% GST is added before presenting to customers. Battery installation labour may be additional — confirm with supplier.
  • Combined OOP = Pi Energy quoted base OOP (inc GST) + battery module cost inc GST. No further STC deduction applies to battery-only additions.
  • Verify all figures with Pi Energy prior to customer presentation.
